# MEDIMESH 🏥

### Better healthcare discovery starts here.

MEDIMESH is a healthcare discovery platform designed to make finding the right healthcare provider simpler and more convenient.

Finding reliable information about hospitals, doctors, and available healthcare services can be confusing and time-consuming. MEDIMESH aims to bring this information together in one place, helping people explore their options and make more informed decisions about their healthcare.

## 🌟 What MEDIMESH Offers

- **Hospital Discovery:** Explore hospitals and learn about their available specialties, facilities, and services.
- **Doctor Discovery:** Find doctors based on their specializations and available professional information.
- **Hospital Comparison:** Compare hospitals side by side to better understand the information available for each provider.
- **Home Visit Services:** Discover doctors who offer scheduled home visits and submit booking requests.
- **Personalized Experience:** Save healthcare options and manage bookings through a dedicated patient experience.
- **Doctor Portal:** Give doctors a dedicated space to manage their professional profiles and home visit requests.
- **Hospital Portal:** Allow hospitals to manage their profiles and share information about their services.
- **Location-Based Discovery:** Help users explore healthcare providers and view their locations on maps.

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make healthcare information easier to discover, understand, and access.

We believe people should be able to explore healthcare options with greater clarity and transparency, without having to search across multiple platforms.

## 💡 Our Vision

We envision a connected healthcare discovery experience where patients, doctors, and hospitals can interact through one unified platform.

MEDIMESH aims to make the process of finding healthcare providers more transparent, accessible, and convenient.

## 🛡️ Our Approach

We believe healthcare decisions should be supported by clear and reliable information.

MEDIMESH aims to distinguish verified information from information that has not yet been verified, clearly communicate when details are unavailable, and avoid presenting unsupported claims as facts.

MEDIMESH supports healthcare discovery and is not a replacement for professional medical advice or emergency medical services.

## 🚀 The Road Ahead

We are continuously working to improve the healthcare discovery experience, strengthen information transparency, make provider searches more useful, and create a more connected experience for patients, doctors, and hospitals.

**MEDIMESH — Connecting people with better healthcare discovery.**
